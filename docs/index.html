Built on high-resolution climate, infrastructure, and demographic data, our system forecasts peak electricity demand using a multi-stream transformer that captures interactions across key grid drivers. An agentic reasoning layer combines these predictions with curated energy reports to deliver explainable, decision-ready insights for California energy planners.

Electricity Demand Forecasting. Experienced through Agentic Retrieval

Agentic Retrieval Decisioning System leveraging Peak Electricity Demand forecast built on high resolution, neighborhood-level data. Incorporating the latest California Utility Reports to provide relevant and summarized information to utility specalists.

01 — Agentic Retrieval

Agentic Reasoning

Built on our micro-climate focused prediction layer and a detailed corpus of California Utility reports, an agentic RAG layer decomposes complex questions into subqueries, retrieves structured and unstructured data, and synthesizes results through an auditable reasoning process. This allows users to explore forecasts, validate assumptions, and generate insights in real time.

DynamoDB · S3 Vector · Tree of Thought · Deep Search
02 — Local, Lived History

Your Weather Experience

Using real-life, hyper-local weather observations compiled by NOAA and synthesized by 1km population data, this model knows how Californians' unique weather experience impacts electricity usage.

URMA 2.5km · WorldPop · xarray · Dask
